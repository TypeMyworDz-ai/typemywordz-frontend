Descript may be the better fit

Choose Descript if you need to produce a podcast or video: edit media by changing its transcript, work with captions, enhance audio, or record a screen as part of the same project. Those production tools are a meaningful part of the product, not extras to dismiss. Check its current plan comparison for transcription hours and other limits.

TypeMyworDz may be the better fit

  • Your deliverable is a transcript, not a finished video. Work in a transcript-first editor and export the text.
  • You want to ask questions about the transcript or an attached file without moving it into a separate chat tool.
  • You transcribe interviews, research, meetings, or dictation and want the editing workflow to stay focused on the words and audio.
  • You need human proofreading for a particular file. Request a quote based on its length and requirements.
