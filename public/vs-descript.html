Where Descript is the better fit

If you are editing a podcast or video — cutting clips, removing filler words from the audio track itself, recording your screen — Descript is a genuine editing tool built for that job, and transcription is just one part of a much bigger product. That is real, useful software if video editing is what you actually need.

Where TypeMyworDz is the better fit

  • You just need the text, not a video timeline. Paying for an editor you won't use is paying for nothing.
  • Your work is interviews, research, or dictation, not podcast or video production.
  • You want an assistant that understands your transcript, so you can ask it questions instead of scrolling to find a quote.
  • You occasionally need certainty, not just a good guess — human proofreading sits on the same account, for the files that matter most.
