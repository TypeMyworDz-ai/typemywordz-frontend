Where Rev is the better fit

If you need one recording done once, with a published per-minute human rate and no account required at all, Rev's pay-as-you-go model is straightforward and well suited to that. Its human transcription is a mature, long-standing product with a clear price tag attached up front.

Where TypeMyworDz is the better fit

  • You transcribe regularly, not just once. Per-minute pricing punishes volume; flat monthly credits reward it.
  • You want AI and human options on the same account — run the AI pass for free-flowing content, and only pay for human proofreading on the parts that truly need it.
  • You want to interrogate your transcript afterwards with an assistant, not just receive a text file.
  • You are watching a monthly budget, not paying per job out of a one-off invoice.
