Things worth knowing

  • Your first 30 minutes are free when you create an account. No card is asked for.
  • Credits you buy are yours for a year, and they add up rather than replacing each other. If a plan runs out, bought credits still work.
  • A plan and credits give you the same access to paid features. Neither is second class.
  • Audio shorter than a minute counts as one minute. Nothing else is rounded.
  • There are no setup fees, no per-user charges, and nothing renews without you choosing it.

What you get for it

Every paid minute includes speaker labels, timestamps, a proofreading editor that plays your audio back as you correct it, export to Word or plain text, and the TypeMyworDz assistant to question or reshape the transcript afterwards. The price on this page is the whole price.
