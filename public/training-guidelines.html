TypeMyworDz human-work guidelines

These standards apply to training exercises and future human-work assignments. They help us deliver accurate, consistent, client-ready work.

Accuracy before speed

Preserve the speaker's meaning and wording. Remove only clear fillers, stutters, duplicated words, false starts, and explicit self-corrections.

Names and research

Keep names, places, organisations, and technical terms consistent. Research distinctive proper nouns when the assignment requires it, but do not rewrite the speaker's meaning.

Speakers and timestamps

Keep speaker changes clear, use the supplied timestamp convention, and check the audio when a word or identity is uncertain.

Client-ready delivery

Follow the client's template and instructions, check the complete document, and submit only work that has been reviewed from beginning to end.

If an assignment has a client-specific brief or template, that brief takes priority over this general guide.
