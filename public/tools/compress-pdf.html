Compress PDF

Make a PDF smaller so it fits in an email or an upload form. Pictures inside are optimised; text stays sharp.

    How it works

    1. Choose a PDF up to 15 MB.
    2. Pick a level: light, recommended or strong.
    3. Press Compress and download. You will see how much smaller it became.

    Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

    Questions

    Will the quality drop?

    Text stays sharp. Pictures inside the PDF are recompressed, so strong compression can soften photos a little.

    What if my PDF cannot get smaller?

    Then you get the original back. We never hand you a bigger file.

    Is it private?

    Yes. Nothing is stored.
