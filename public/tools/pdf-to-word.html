PDF to Word converter

Turn a PDF into a Word document you can edit. Text, headings, tables and pictures are carried across as closely as possible.

How it works

  1. Choose a PDF that contains selectable text, up to 15 MB.
  2. Press Convert and download.
  3. Open the DOCX in Word and edit.

Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

Questions

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

A scan is a picture of a page, so there is no text to move into Word. Use PDF to JPG for scans, or upload the pages to TypeMyworDz to have them transcribed.

Will tables and columns survive?

In most documents, yes. Very complex layouts may need a little tidying in Word.

Is it free and private?

Yes. No account is needed and your file is deleted right after conversion.
