Merge PDF files

Put several PDFs into one document, in the order you choose. Pictures can be added too and become pages.

    How it works

    1. Choose two or more PDFs (up to 12 files, 15 MB each).
    2. Use the arrows to put them in the right order.
    3. Press Merge and download the single PDF.

    Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

    Questions

    How many files can I merge?

    Up to 12 files at a time, each up to 15 MB.

    Can I merge pictures with PDFs?

    Yes. JPG and PNG pictures become one page each.

    Is it private?

    Yes. Files are merged in memory and deleted as soon as your download is ready.
