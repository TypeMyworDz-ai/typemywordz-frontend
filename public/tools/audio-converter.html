Audio converter

Change a recording into MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG or FLAC, shrink a heavy file, or pull the sound out of a video. Ideal for getting a recording ready for transcription.

    How it works

    1. Choose an audio or video file (up to 40 MB).
    2. Pick the format and quality. 96 kbps MP3 is small and clear for speech.
    3. Press Convert and download.

    Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

    Questions

    Which settings are best for speech?

    MP3 at 64 or 96 kbps, mono, gives a small file that is still clear. It also transcribes well.

    Can I take the audio out of a video?

    Yes. Choose an MP4, MOV or WebM file and convert it to MP3.

    What is the size limit?

    40 MB per file, and 20 conversions an hour.
