Split PDF

Pull the pages you need out of a PDF, cut it into parts, or save every page as a separate file.

    How it works

    1. Choose a PDF (up to 15 MB and 200 pages).
    2. Pick what to do and, if needed, type the pages, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10.
    3. Press Split. Several files download as one ZIP.

    Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

    Questions

    How do I write the page numbers?

    Type pages and ranges separated by commas, such as 1-3, 5, 8-10. A range with no end, such as 8-, goes to the last page.

    What is the difference between the options?

    Extract pages gives one PDF with just those pages. Split into parts gives one PDF per range. Every page gives a ZIP with a file per page.

    Do you keep my PDF?

    No. It is deleted right after the download is ready.
