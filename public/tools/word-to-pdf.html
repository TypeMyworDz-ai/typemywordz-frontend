Word to PDF converter

Turn a Word document into a PDF that looks the same on every device. Choose your file, press convert, and the PDF downloads straight away.

    How it works

    1. Choose a Word file (DOCX, DOC, RTF, ODT or TXT) up to 15 MB.
    2. Press Convert and download.
    3. Open the PDF anywhere, or send it to someone who does not have Word.

    Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

    Questions

    Is this Word to PDF converter really free?

    Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no page limit beyond the 15 MB file size.

    Will my formatting change?

    The page layout, fonts, tables and pictures are kept. If your document uses an unusual font that is not installed here, a close match is used.

    Do you keep my document?

    No. The file is converted in memory and deleted as soon as your download is ready.
