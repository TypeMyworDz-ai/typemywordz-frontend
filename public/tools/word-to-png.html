Word to PNG converter

Turn the pages of a Word document into sharp PNG pictures. PNG keeps text crisp, which makes it a good choice for documents.

    How it works

    1. Choose a Word file up to 15 MB.
    2. Press Convert. Several pages download as one ZIP.
    3. Use the PNG files wherever you need them.

    Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

    Questions

    PNG or JPG for a document?

    PNG keeps small text sharper. JPG gives smaller files. Both are available here.

    Is there a page limit?

    Up to 40 pages at a time on the free tool.

    Do you store my file?

    No. It is deleted right after conversion.
