Word to JPG converter

Save every page of a Word document as a JPG picture, ready to share in a chat, post online or drop into a slide.

    How it works

    1. Choose a Word file up to 15 MB.
    2. Press Convert. One page gives one JPG; several pages give a ZIP of JPGs.
    3. Download and use the pictures anywhere.

    Your file is converted on our server and deleted as soon as the download is ready. We never keep it, read it, or use it to train AI. The free tool allows 20 conversions an hour.

    Questions

    How are multi-page documents handled?

    Each page becomes its own JPG, and they are packed into one ZIP file for download.

    What is the picture quality?

    Pages are drawn at about 150 to 200 dpi, which is sharp on screen and fine for most printing.

    Is it free?

    Yes, with no account and no watermark.
