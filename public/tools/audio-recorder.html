Free audio recorder

A very small voice recorder that does one job well. Press record, pause when you need to, and keep a compact file that is ready to transcribe. Use it in your browser, install it on your phone, or download the tiny Windows program that needs no installation and no browser.

What it does

  • Record, pause and resume, with a running timer and a live level meter.
  • Saves as you record, so a crash or a closed tab never loses your recording.
  • Skips long silences while recording, if you switch it on.
  • Mark important moments; the marks are listed with your recording.
  • Upload to TypeMyworDz in one press and get your transcript.
  • Installs on a phone from the browser, and there is a small Windows program that needs no browser.

How it works

  1. Open the recorder in your browser, install it on your phone, or download the Windows program and double-click it.
  2. Press Record. Use Pause to stop for a moment and Mark to flag an important moment.
  3. Press Stop, listen back and save. Then upload the file to TypeMyworDz to get the transcript.

Questions

How big is the Windows program?

Under 200 KB, a single file. Double-click it and record. There is no installer and no account. Windows may show a blue "protected your PC" notice because the program is new: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open the recorder in your phone browser and choose Install or Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. It is recorded and kept on your own device. Nothing leaves it unless you press Upload to TypeMyworDz. The recording is also saved as you go, so a crash or a closed tab does not lose it.

Which file type does it save?

The browser version saves WebM, OGG or M4A depending on your browser. The Windows program saves a small 32 kbps MP3 by default, in Documents, TypeMyworDz Recordings, and you can pick another folder or quality in its settings. TypeMyworDz and our Audio Converter accept all of them,.

How do I get a transcript?

Save the recording, then upload it on TypeMyworDz. You get an accurate, editable transcript and can ask AI questions about it.
