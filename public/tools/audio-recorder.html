Free audio recorder

A very small voice recorder that does one job well. Press record, pause when you need to, and save a compact file that is ready to transcribe. Use it in your browser, or download it as a single 7 KB file that works offline.

Open the recorder Download (7 KB)

What it does

  • Record, pause and resume, with a running timer and a live level meter.
  • Mark important moments; the marks are listed with your recording.
  • Choose a quality: smallest file for speech, standard, or high.
  • Records in mono with noise suppression, so files stay small and voices stay clear.
  • Keeps your screen awake while recording, and warns you before you close an unsaved recording.

How it works

  1. Open the recorder, or download the file and open it from your computer or phone.
  2. Press Record. Use Pause to stop for a moment and Mark to flag an important moment.
  3. Press Stop, listen back and save. Then upload the file to TypeMyworDz to get the transcript.

Questions

How big is the download?

About 7 KB, a single file. There is nothing to install and no account.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. It is recorded and kept on your own device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to upload the saved file to TypeMyworDz.

Which file type does it save?

Your browser decides: WebM or OGG on Chrome, Edge and Firefox, M4A on Safari. TypeMyworDz and our Audio Converter accept all of them.

How do I get a transcript?

Save the recording, then upload it on TypeMyworDz. You get an accurate, editable transcript and can ask AI questions about it.
