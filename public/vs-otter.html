Choose Otter if your work begins in live meetings and you value meeting capture, meeting notes, and its integrations with tools such as Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams. TypeMyworDz may be the better fit

  • You sometimes need a person to proofread. Human proofreading can be requested for work that needs more than an AI pass.
