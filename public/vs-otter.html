Where Otter is the better fit

If you live in back-to-back video meetings and want live, real-time captions inside the call itself, Otter’s meeting integrations (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams) are more mature than anything TypeMyworDz offers today. Where TypeMyworDz is the better fit
